## Why Datly 1.0 and xdatly v1

Datly 1.0 transcribes DQL into typed reader/writer components and owns ordinary binding, SQL/DML, sparse field presence, native predicates, CAS/lease mutations, transactions, selectors, and cubes. That replaces the SDK0 `datly.Service`/`repository`/`view` components and hand-authored persistence SQL in Core. Business Go belongs in predicates, codecs, lifecycle hooks, or genuine custom orchestration; the reader/writer contract and framework plumbing must come from stock `datly transcribe`.

Unified `github.com/viant/xdatly` v1 supplies the component and hook contracts expected by Datly 1.0. The old split `xdatly/handler` and `xdatly/types/*` modules conflict with the unified v1 package tree. Core, nested and Agently module files now select unified xdatly v1; split-module requirements are removed. Keep the local `viant/xdatly` checkout on `v1`; do not substitute the older `main` package tree.
